100 Days of Art

Make art every day for 100 days. Each morning you get a short email. Each day is one simple session: say how you feel, get a prompt made for that mood, make something, tap done. Your tracker fills up as you go.

Start the challenge

One daily session, four little steps

1Feel
Pick a face and your colours.
2Prompt
Get a prompt that fits your mood and time.
3Make
Make it on your own paper. A timer helps.
4Done
Note what you made. Your circle fills in.

"During COVID I felt stuck, depressed and anxious. My wife Rebecca suggested I make art every day for 100 days. Daily creativity gave me purpose, something to wake up to, and somewhere to put what I was feeling. If it worked for me, I believe it can work for anyone."

David Zak, artist and author of the 100 Days of Art Workbook
